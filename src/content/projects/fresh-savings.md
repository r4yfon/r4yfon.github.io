FreshSavings helps households waste less food: track what's in the pantry, cook what's about to expire, and share the surplus with neighbours. It's a solo rebuild of a [university group project](https://github.com/r4yfon/freshsavings) I worked on in 2023, remade in React, TypeScript and Supabase. I scaffolded it with [lovable.dev](https://lovable.dev) to move fast, then reworked it by hand. It's a frontend and UX prototype: the backend was scaffolded but never wired up, so the focus here is the interface.

## Designing for awkward quantities

Pantry items don't share a unit: you own *6* eggs, *a kilogram* of rice and *2 litres* of milk. Quantities also have to stay consistent as they move through the app, which made them the hardest UX problem here.

- **Unit picker per item:** every pantry item pairs a count with a unit (pieces, kilograms or litres), defaulting to pieces with a two-week expiry so the common case takes one tap.
- **Cooking with what you have:** the recipe generator lets you pick pantry items with a −/+ stepper that can't go above what you actually own.
- **Sharing with guardrails:** when you share food to the Community Kitchen, you can't offer more than your pantry holds. Shared listings can add bags and boxes as units, because that's how people hand food over.

## Features

- **Pantry:** add, edit and remove items, with expiry dates and categories.
- **Community Kitchen:** share surplus food with a pickup location and an "available until" date. Items expiring within two weeks are flagged for sharing first.
- **Recipe Generator:** recipe ideas from what's in your pantry, generated by AI.
- **Dark mode and responsive layout.**

## Tech stack

- **Frontend:** React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui
- **Backend (scaffolded, not wired up):** Supabase
- **State/Data:** React Query
